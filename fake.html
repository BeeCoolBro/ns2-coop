Birdwatching for everyone, one quiet morning at a time

We're a small volunteer club that meets for easy walks along the creek trail and the old orchard. No experience needed, and we always have spare binoculars.

Regular walks

Saturday Creek Walk

Saturdays, 8:00 AM. About 2 miles on a flat, shaded trail. Meet at the footbridge by the picnic shelter.

Orchard Evenings

First Wednesday of the month, an hour before sunset. Good for swallows, bluebirds and the occasional owl.

Winter Feeder Count

December through February. Count the birds at your own feeder for fifteen minutes and send us your tally.

New to birding?

  • Start with the birds at your window. Learning ten common ones makes the rare ones stand out.
  • Binoculars around 8x42 are a comfortable all-round choice. Borrow a pair from us before you buy.
  • Listen as much as you look. Most birds are heard long before they're seen.
  • Keep a simple notebook: date, place, weather and what you saw.
  • Walk slowly and stop often. The trail rewards patience.

About the club

Hollow Creek Birding Club started with a handful of neighbours and a shared field guide. We still keep things simple: walks are free, everyone is welcome, and we look after the nest boxes along the trail together. Families and first-timers are always welcome.
